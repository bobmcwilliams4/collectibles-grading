Frequently Asked Questions

Is EPOCGS free to use?

Yes! The basic version is free with 10 AI grades per month. Premium plans unlock unlimited grading and advanced features.

Do I need an internet connection?

You can manage your collection offline, but AI grading requires an internet connection to access our multi-model consensus system.

How accurate is the AI grading?

Our multi-model consensus (Claude + Gemini + GPT-4) achieves 95%+ correlation with professional grading services like CGC.

Can I sync between devices?

Absolutely! Sign in with the same account on desktop and mobile to keep your collection synced across all devices.

What file formats are supported for grading?

We support JPG, PNG, HEIC, and WebP images. For best results, use high-resolution photos with good lighting.

Need Help Installing?

Our support team is here to help you get started. Check out our documentation or reach out directly.
